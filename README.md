Brief
An application to monitor the wellbeing of individuals. Utilising SPANE-B and SPANE-N metrics.

Problem Statement
Many graduates are left to work in isolation on projects and have to wait for EC checkins before they can comfortable reach out to the EC coaches about how they maybe feeling. This means issues maybe left unresolved, especially when busy trying to upskill/catch up with fast paced projects. How can we use technology to improve the wellbeing of graduates in such conditions?

Solution
Energy App is an application to aims to monitor the wellbeing of graduates in real time. This will allow EC coaches to monitor whether graduates feel negative or positive energy in a given time. Grads with a high negative energy level will be flagged to EC Coaches, and Coaches will be prompted to set a meeting with graduate to see what they can do to help.

Tech Stack:
- Next.js (v10) for Frontend
- Firebase for Backend
- Node.js (v18)
